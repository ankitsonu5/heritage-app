# Heritage Diagnostics — Account Deletion Request

Heritage Diagnostics users may request deletion of their app account and associated eligible data.

## How to request deletion

Email **heritagediagnosticsvaranasi@gmail.com** with:

- Subject: **Heritage App Account Deletion**
- The mobile number registered with the app
- The account holder's name

Heritage Diagnostics may contact the requester to verify account ownership. After verification, the account and eligible associated data will be deleted or anonymised. Some diagnostic, transaction, security, or service records may be retained where required for medical-record, accounting, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or other legal obligations. Retained data will be restricted from ordinary use and kept only for the required period.

Deleting the mobile app does not automatically delete an account.

For privacy questions, contact **heritagediagnosticsvaranasi@gmail.com**.
