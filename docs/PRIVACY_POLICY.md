# Privacy Policy — Heritage Diagnostics

**Effective date:** 31 July 2026  
**Last updated:** 31 July 2026

Heritage Diagnostics, Varanasi ("Heritage Diagnostics", "we", "our", or "us") operates the Heritage Diagnostics mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, why it is used, when it may be disclosed, and the choices available to users.

## 1. Information we collect

We may collect the following information when a patient or authorised staff member uses the app:

- **Account and contact information:** name, mobile number, login identifier, age (if provided), city/village, and collection address.
- **Health and diagnostic information:** prescription photographs or PDF files, requested diagnostic tests, sample-collection details, test reports, order status, and related service records.
- **Transaction information:** order amount, selected payment mode, and whether payment was collected. The app does not collect or store card numbers, bank-account credentials, or UPI PINs.
- **Staff information:** staff name, username, role, phone number, work zone, availability, and actions taken on an order.
- **Device and notification information:** Firebase installation identifier, push-notification token, app version, and basic device/app metadata needed to deliver notifications and maintain the service.
- **Technical information:** server logs such as request time, IP address, error information, and security events may be processed by our hosting and infrastructure providers.

Passwords are stored only as one-way password hashes. We do not store plain-text account passwords.

## 2. Camera, gallery, files, and notifications

- Camera access is used only when a user chooses to photograph a prescription.
- Gallery/file access is used only when a user chooses an existing prescription image or PDF, or downloads a diagnostic report.
- Notification permission is used to provide order, sample-collection, and report updates.

The app does not use camera or gallery access for advertising, facial recognition, or background surveillance.

## 3. How we use information

We use information to:

- create and secure patient and staff accounts;
- receive prescriptions and provide diagnostic/home sample-collection services;
- allow authorised PRO, collection-agent, laboratory, and administrator staff to process orders;
- contact users regarding appointments, sample collection, payments, and reports;
- deliver in-app and push notifications;
- provide reports and order history;
- prevent fraud, investigate errors, maintain security, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell personal or health information, and we do not use it for third-party behavioural advertising.

## 4. Disclosure of information

Information may be disclosed only as needed to:

- authorised Heritage Diagnostics personnel who require it to perform their assigned role;
- service providers supporting database hosting, application hosting, file delivery, SMS/email communication, and push notifications, including MongoDB Atlas and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging;
- government, regulatory, law-enforcement, or legal authorities where disclosure is required by applicable law or necessary to protect users, the public, or our legal rights;
- a successor organisation if the service is reorganised, subject to applicable law and appropriate protection of user information.

These providers process information to deliver services on our behalf and are not authorised by us to use it for unrelated advertising.

## 5. Data security

The production app communicates with our service using HTTPS encryption. We use access controls, role-based permissions, authentication tokens, password hashing, and restricted staff access. No system is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

## 6. Retention and deletion

We retain account, diagnostic, transaction, and service records only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service and meet security, dispute-resolution, accounting, medical-record, and legal obligations.

Users may request deletion of their account and associated data by emailing **heritagediagnosticsvaranasi@gmail.com** with the subject **Heritage App Account Deletion** and the registered mobile number. We may verify identity before completing the request. Information that must be retained for a legitimate legal, regulatory, fraud-prevention, or medical-record obligation will be isolated from ordinary use and retained only for the required period. Remaining eligible data will be deleted or anonymised.

Deleting the app from a device does not by itself delete an account or server-held records.

## 7. User choices

Users may deny camera, photo/file, or notification permissions through Android settings. Denying camera or file access prevents prescription upload through that method; denying notifications prevents push alerts but does not prevent account access. Users may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of eligible information.

## 8. Children

The service is not directed to children for independent use. A parent or lawful guardian should create and manage any service request for a minor and provide only information necessary for diagnostic services.

## 9. Medical disclaimer

The app facilitates diagnostic-test ordering, home sample collection, status updates, and report delivery. It does not replace a qualified medical professional, provide emergency services, or independently diagnose or prescribe treatment. Users should consult an appropriate healthcare professional regarding reports or medical concerns.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the service, legal requirements, or data practices change. The current version will show its effective and last-updated dates. Material changes will be communicated through an appropriate in-app or service notice where required.
