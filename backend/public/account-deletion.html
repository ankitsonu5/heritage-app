Delete your account

Heritage Diagnostics mobile application

Heritage Diagnostics users may request deletion of their app account and associated eligible data without reinstalling or signing in to the app.

How to request deletion

  1. Email heritagediagnosticsvaranasi@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject Heritage App Account Deletion.
  3. Include the mobile number registered with the app and the account holder's name.

Email deletion request

Verification and deletion

We may contact the requester to verify account ownership. After verification, the account will be disabled from login and eligible associated data will be deleted or anonymised.

Data that may be retained

Diagnostic, transaction, security, or service records may be retained where required for medical-record, accounting, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or other legal obligations. Retained data will be restricted from ordinary use and kept only for the required period.

Important: Deleting the mobile app from a phone does not automatically delete the account or server-held records.
