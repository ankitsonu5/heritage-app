Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 July 2026
Last updated: 31 July 2026

Heritage Diagnostics, Varanasi ("Heritage Diagnostics", "we", "our", or "us") operates the Heritage Diagnostics mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, why it is used, when it may be disclosed, and the choices available to users.

1. Information we collect

  • Account and contact information: name, mobile number, login identifier, age (if provided), city/village, and collection address.
  • Health and diagnostic information: prescription photographs or PDF files, requested diagnostic tests, sample-collection details, test reports, order status, and related service records.
  • Transaction information: order amount, payment mode, and whether payment was collected. We do not collect or store card numbers, bank credentials, or UPI PINs.
  • Staff information: staff name, username, role, phone number, work zone, availability, and actions taken on an order.
  • Device and technical information: Firebase installation identifier, push token, app version, request time, IP address, error information, and security events needed to operate and protect the service.

Passwords are stored only as one-way password hashes. We do not store plain-text account passwords.

2. Camera, gallery, files, and notifications

  • Camera access is used only when a user chooses to photograph a prescription.
  • Gallery or file access is used only when a user selects a prescription image or PDF, or downloads a diagnostic report. On supported Android versions, the system photo picker limits access to selected images.
  • Notification permission is used for order, sample-collection, and report updates.

We do not use camera or gallery access for advertising, facial recognition, or background surveillance.

3. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts; receive prescriptions; provide diagnostic and home sample-collection services; allow authorised staff to process orders; contact users about appointments, payments and reports; deliver notifications; prevent fraud; investigate errors; maintain security; and comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell personal or health information and do not use it for third-party behavioural advertising.

4. Disclosure

Information may be disclosed only to authorised Heritage Diagnostics personnel; service providers supporting database hosting, application hosting, file delivery, communications and push notifications, including MongoDB Atlas and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging; legal authorities where required; or a successor organisation subject to applicable protections.

5. Data security

The production app communicates using HTTPS encryption. We use authentication, role-based access, password hashing, restricted staff access, and security controls. No system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Retention and deletion

We retain account, diagnostic, transaction, and service records only as reasonably necessary to provide the service and meet security, dispute-resolution, accounting, medical-record, and legal obligations.

Users may request deletion by following our Account Deletion instructions. We may verify identity. Data that must be retained for legitimate legal, regulatory, fraud-prevention, or medical-record obligations will be isolated from ordinary use and retained only for the required period. Other eligible data will be deleted or anonymised.

Deleting the app from a device does not itself delete the account or server-held records.

7. User choices

Users may deny camera, photo/file, or notification permissions through Android settings. Denying a permission may prevent the related feature from working. Users may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of eligible information.

8. Children

The service is not directed to children for independent use. A parent or lawful guardian should manage requests for a minor and provide only information necessary for diagnostic services.

9. Medical disclaimer

The app facilitates diagnostic-test ordering, home sample collection, status updates, and report delivery. It does not replace a qualified medical professional, provide emergency services, or independently diagnose or prescribe treatment.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when the service, legal requirements, or data practices change. The current page will show its effective and last-updated dates.
